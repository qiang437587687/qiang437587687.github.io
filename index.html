Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Apple Music Box ("we", "us", or "our") is a third‑party dedicated client built for Apple Music. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our application and official website.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our app does not actively collect your personal identification information, including but not limited to your name, phone number, address, ID number, or other sensitive private data. We never upload your local music data, playback history, or device information to any server without your explicit consent.

2. Information We Collect Automatically

To ensure the normal operation of the application, we may automatically collect anonymous basic device information and routine operational logs. All collected data is used solely for crash repair, feature optimization, and user experience improvement. It is never used for commercial marketing or shared with third parties.

3. Use of Information

All information we obtain is used exclusively for:

• Maintaining and optimizing the proper functioning of Apple Music Box;

• Fixing bugs and improving product features;

• Enhancing the user interaction experience.

4. Information Storage & Security

All user local data is stored only on your personal device. We employ standard technical security measures to protect application data and prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or loss.

5. Third‑Party Services

This application is a third‑party client and is not affiliated with Apple Inc. The music service is provided by Apple Music. Users' subscriptions and account activities are subject to Apple's official privacy policy. We do not obtain any of your Apple account private information.
